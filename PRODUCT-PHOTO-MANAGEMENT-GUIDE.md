# 📸 Product Photo Management - Admin Guide

## ✅ **IMPLEMENTED: Complete Photo Management System**

### **📋 Features Added:**

#### **🖼️ Main Product Listing (`/admin/products`):**
- **Photo Preview:** Each product shows current main photo or placeholder
- **Photo Status:** Indicates "1 photo" or "No photos" on hover
- **Quick Actions:** 
  - 👁️ **View** - See product on website
  - 📷 **Photos** - Direct link to photo management  
  - ✏️ **Edit** - Full product editing
  - 🗑️ **Delete** - Remove product

#### **📝 Edit Product Page (`/admin/products/[id]`):**
- **Complete Photo Gallery:** View all uploaded photos in grid layout
- **Main Photo Selection:** Mark any photo as the "Main" product image
- **Upload New Photos:** Drag & drop or click to upload (PNG, JPG, WEBP up to 10MB)
- **Delete Photos:** Remove unwanted photos with confirmation
- **Real-time Preview:** See changes immediately

### **🎯 How to Use:**

#### **Adding Photos to Existing Products:**
1. **Go to:** `/admin/products`
2. **Find Product:** Use search or browse the grid
3. **Click Photo Button:** 📷 blue camera icon
4. **Upload Photos:** Click "Add Product Photo" area
5. **Set Main Photo:** Click "Set Main" on preferred image
6. **Save Changes:** Photos are saved immediately

#### **Managing Multiple Photos:**
- **Upload Multiple:** Add several photos for different angles
- **Main Photo:** The "Main" photo appears in product listings
- **Gallery Photos:** Additional photos show in product detail pages
- **Delete Photos:** Hover and click trash icon to remove
- **Reorder:** Set any photo as main photo anytime

### **📐 Photo Requirements:**
- **Formats:** PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP
- **Size:** Maximum 10MB per photo
- **Dimensions:** Minimum 800x600px recommended
- **Orientation:** Square or landscape works best
- **Quality:** High resolution for best results

### **🔄 Photo Workflow:**
1. **Upload** → Photos saved to storage
2. **Preview** → Immediate display in admin
3. **Set Main** → Choose primary product image
4. **Display** → Auto-shows on website
5. **Edit/Delete** → Manage anytime

### **💡 Pro Tips:**
- **Multiple Angles:** Upload front, back, detail shots
- **Consistent Style:** Use similar lighting/background
- **Main Photo:** Choose most appealing for listings
- **File Names:** Use descriptive names for organization
- **Backup:** System handles storage automatically

### **🌐 Website Integration:**
- **Product Listings:** Show main photo automatically
- **Product Pages:** Display full photo gallery
- **Mobile Responsive:** Works on all devices
- **Fast Loading:** Optimized image delivery
