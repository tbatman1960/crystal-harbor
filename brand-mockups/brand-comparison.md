# Crystal Harbor Trading Company - Brand Variations

## Overview
All variations use **Navy Blue + Silver** as the trustworthy foundation, with different vibrant accent colors for energy and fun.

**Typography:** Inter (body) + Poppins (headings)
**Foundation:** Navy (#1E3A8A) + Silver (#94A3B8) + Charcoal (#374151) + Off-White (#F8FAFC)

## Visual Elements Across All Variations

### Homepage Layout
- **Header:** Navy background, white logo, silver navigation
- **Hero Section:** Navy gradient with white text and colorful CTA
- **Product Categories:** White cards with colored icons on off-white background
- **Overall Feel:** Clean, modern, mobile-friendly

### Logo Approach
- Simple, text-based design
- Professional typography (Poppins)
- Accent colors highlight "Harbor" or parts of the name
- Tagline: "Custom Printed Products Made Simple"

### UI Elements
- **Buttons:** Accent color(s) with white text
- **Links:** Silver that changes to accent on hover
- **Cards:** White backgrounds with subtle shadows
- **Icons:** Circular accent-colored backgrounds
